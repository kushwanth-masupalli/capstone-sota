5.4.2 Ablation of linguistic features in report
semantic complexity
As shown in Figure 2d, we also validate the report semantic
complexity component ti in the proposed medical prior feature
vector. Speciﬁcally, we ablate its three components, including
report length, medical entity count, and medical relation count. The
full model achieves the best BLEU-4 and METEOR scores, and the
results show that removing any component degrades performance,
conﬁrming the usefulness of modeling report semantic complexity.
